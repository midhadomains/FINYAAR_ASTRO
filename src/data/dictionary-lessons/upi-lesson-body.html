UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India's instant, round-the-clock payment system that lets you move money directly between two bank accounts using a mobile phone. It is built and run by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), works 24x7 including weekends and holidays, and settles in seconds. Instead of sharing your account number and IFSC code, you use a simple Virtual Payment Address (VPA), also called a UPI ID, and authorise each payment with a secret UPI PIN.

What UPI is

UPI is a real-time interbank transfer rail managed by NPCI, the same body that runs RuPay and IMPS. It links your bank account to a payment app of your choice — a bank app or a third-party app — and lets that app initiate credits and debits on your behalf. The clever part is the Virtual Payment Address (VPA): a short handle such as name@bank that maps to your account behind the scenes. You share the VPA, not your actual account number, so payments stay convenient without exposing sensitive banking details. UPI sits on top of your existing savings account, so money never leaves the banking system — there is no separate wallet balance to load. One phone can link several accounts, and one account can be reached through many VPAs.

How UPI works: VPA and UPI PIN

To pay, you enter the receiver's VPA or scan their QR code, type the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN — a 4 or 6 digit secret set inside the app and tied to your debit card and account. The request travels through the app to NPCI, which routes it to both banks; the sender bank debits and the receiver bank credits, usually within seconds. A vital safety rule follows from the design: the UPI PIN is only ever needed to SEND money, never to receive it. If anyone asks you to enter your PIN, approve a collect request, or scan a QR code in order to receive cash, it is a scam. Receiving money requires nothing more than sharing your VPA or QR.

Features, limits and costs

UPI is free for person-to-person transfers for ordinary users — sending money to family or friends costs nothing. It runs 24x7, supports QR-based merchant payments, autopay mandates for bills and SIP instalments, and even linking of RuPay credit cards. Per-transaction and daily limits exist, but they are set and revised by NPCI and your bank, so they vary by account and by app — check your bank for current values rather than assuming a fixed number. Staying safe on UPI

Because UPI is instant and irreversible, safe habits matter. Never share your UPI PIN, one-time passwords, or app login with anyone, including callers claiming to be from your bank. Remember that you never key in a PIN to get money. Verify the receiver name shown before confirming a payment, be wary of unexpected collect requests, and avoid scanning random QR codes sent by strangers. Keeping most of your balance in a separate account and only a working sum in the UPI-linked one, alongside a healthy emergency fund and disciplined budgeting, limits the damage if something goes wrong.

Worked example
Anjali runs a small tea-and-snacks stall in Pune. She takes about ₹3,00,000 in customer payments each month across many small bills. She is deciding whether to keep pushing customers toward UPI QR acceptance or to install a card machine that charges a Merchant Discount Rate. She wants to know how much each choice actually costs her.

Step 1 — Set out the two options. On UPI QR, small-value person-to-merchant payments to a stall like hers are effectively free — there is no MDR on typical low-value UPI collections. On a card machine, the acquirer charges an MDR; assume a blended 1.5% on the full amount she routes through cards.

Step 2 — Cost of the UPI route. With no MDR, her fee is ₹3,00,000 × 0% = ₹0. Using the net-credit formula, Net credit = ₹3,00,000 × (1 − 0%) = ₹3,00,000. She keeps every rupee.

Step 3 — Cost of the card route. Suppose customers put the same ₹3,00,000 on cards. MDR cost = ₹3,00,000 × 1.5% = ₹4,500. Net credit = ₹3,00,000 × (1 − 0.015) = ₹2,95,500. The card machine also carries a monthly rental, say ₹300, so her true card cost is closer to ₹4,800 a month.

For a thin-margin food stall, steering customers to UPI QR is plainly cheaper, and knowing the net-credit formula lets her see the true cost before signing up for any machine.

Key takeaways

  • UPI is India's instant, 24x7 bank-to-bank payment system built and run by NPCI.
  • You pay using a VPA (UPI ID) or QR code and authorise with a secret UPI PIN.
  • The UPI PIN is only for sending money — you never need it to receive, so a PIN request to receive is a scam.
  • Person-to-person transfers are free for users; per-transaction and daily limits are set by NPCI and your bank and can change.
  • For merchants, small-value UPI is largely free, while cards charge an MDR that reduces net credit.

Frequently asked questions

Is UPI free to use?

For ordinary users, sending money person-to-person is free. Some merchant or wallet-loading scenarios can attract charges, but everyday UPI transfers between people cost nothing.

Do I ever enter my UPI PIN to receive money?

No. The UPI PIN is only needed to send money. To receive, you simply share your VPA or QR. Anyone asking for a PIN to receive funds is trying to defraud you.

What is a VPA or UPI ID?

A Virtual Payment Address is a short handle such as name@bank that maps to your bank account. You share it instead of your account number and IFSC code.

What are the transfer limits on UPI?

Per-transaction and daily limits apply, but they are set and periodically revised by NPCI and individual banks. They differ by account and app, so check your own bank for the current figures.

Can I link more than one bank account?

Yes. A single UPI app can link several accounts, and you choose which account to pay from for each transaction. Each account still uses your device and UPI PIN for security.

Is a UPI payment reversible if I pay the wrong person?

UPI is instant and generally irreversible, so always verify the receiver name before confirming. If you pay in error, raise a complaint with your app and bank quickly, though recovery is not guaranteed.
